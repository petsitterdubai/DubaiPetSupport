About Dubai Pet Support

Your Trusted Pet Care Partner in the United Arab Emirates

Our Mission

At Dubai Pet Support, we believe that every pet deserves exceptional care, love, and attention. Founded with a deep passion for animal welfare, our mission is to provide peace of mind to pet owners across Dubai and the wider UAE. We understand that your pets are not just animals; they are cherished family members. That is why we have built a team of dedicated, background-checked, and highly trained pet care professionals who treat your pets as if they were their own.

Why Choose Us?

  • 24/7 Availability: Pets don't keep office hours, and neither do we. Our services are available around the clock, 365 days a year.
  • UAE-Wide Coverage: Based in Dubai, we proudly extend our premium pet sitting, boarding, and walking services to all emirates.
  • Tailored Care Plans: No two pets are alike. We customize feeding, exercise, and medication routines to match your pet's exact needs.
  • Transparent Communication: Receive real-time photo and video updates during every visit, so you never have to wonder how your pet is doing.
